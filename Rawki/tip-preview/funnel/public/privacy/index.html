Webinar privacy notice

Current preview. Tickets and calendar files are created in your browser. Email delivery, server-side registration, payments and live chat are not connected. Completing this preview does not send your details to a registration or email service.

Updated November 9, 2026.

Your saved ticket on this device

When you finish your ticket, necessary browser storage remembers your name, email address, selected class, ticket reference and registration choices so the ticket is available when you return in the same browser. These details are kept in local storage under tip.saved-ticket.v1. The necessary tip_ticket_saved cookie only marks that a ticket has been saved; it does not contain your name or email. This storage does not enable analytics or advertising and does not require those optional categories.

The saved copy expires 30 days after your selected class ends. Use “Start a new ticket” below your ticket to remove it, or clear this site’s browser data. A different browser or device will not have this copy. Private browsing and blocked storage may prevent it from being remembered. Downloaded ticket and calendar files remain wherever you save them, even if you clear the browser copy.

Proposed production policy — not active in this preview

The following describes the planned connected service. It does not mean that the database, CRM, email, payment or measurement services described below are currently connected.

Your registration and CRM record

We collect your first name, email, event-contact agreement, registration time, and campaign/source labels from the registration link. These records let us save your place, answer questions, and operate the webinar. Your confirmation email includes your class details and free private Inner Circle invitation. Registration does not require optional analytics or advertising. Future Saturday invitations are a separate, optional checkbox; it starts unchecked. Existing registrants are not automatically subscribed to those invitations.

Class emails and weekly invitations

Our SootyEdge email service sends your registration confirmation using its configured Resend sender. An email accepted by the provider may still be delayed or filtered by your email service. We save a delivery reference and queue status so retries do not intentionally send the same confirmation or weekly invitation twice.

If you choose future Saturday invitations, we keep that preference and may email you about the upcoming Saturday class from Thursday morning Eastern time. Use the preference link in any webinar email to stop future invitations. That link uses an opaque signed identifier, without your email address. You can register for an individual class without opting into future invitations. Opening a preference page does not unsubscribe you until you confirm the change.

Optional measurement

If you allow analytics, we use a random browser-session identifier and record page views, seat-button clicks, form starts, section views, scroll milestones, chart selections, calendar clicks, software-link clicks, offer views, and checkout-button clicks. We connect a completed registration to its source and avoid counting duplicate registrations twice. A checkout click is interest, not a purchase.

Google Analytics and Meta advertising pixels run only when their respective connection is configured and the corresponding tracking category is enabled under your cookie choices and the regional defaults below. On recognized campaign links, validated source labels are kept for registration and the query string is removed before configured tags load on eligible public pages. Links containing unknown or private-looking values remain excluded from these tags. Google and Meta tags are excluded from private confirmation pages; permitted activity there stays in our own measurement system under a generic page label. Your name, email, private registration reference, form contents, and trading-account information are not sent as pixel event properties. If you allow advertising, a validated Meta ad-click identifier may be stored in an advertising cookie. This site does not record your keystrokes or replay your browsing session. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Optional checkout attribution

If you allow analytics, a registrant software payment link can include an independently generated purchase reference. This reference is separate from your private registration reference and does not contain your name or email. It lets us match a verified Stripe payment to the registration and campaign source in our records.

When analytics is enabled, this optional checkout preparation can set an HttpOnly privacy-binding cookie, which website scripts cannot read. It binds the checkout reference to your browser’s current analytics choice. The direct payment link remains available when optional attribution is off or unavailable. Verified Stripe payment and refund records are handled separately from checkout clicks.

Verified purchase returns

A verified software checkout return can set a signed, HttpOnly receipt cookie for up to 15 minutes so we can confirm your payment and show the access instructions. It contains an opaque order identifier and amount, with no name, email, TradingView username, or raw Stripe payment identifier. If you allow the relevant optional category, Google Analytics or Meta can receive a purchase event with the software price, currency, and that opaque order identifier. Google also receives the tax amount. Optional session storage helps avoid repeating the same purchase event for each service. Visiting an unverified checkout or next-steps page does not count as a purchase.

Cookie policy and your choices

For visits identified by our hosting provider as originating in a U.S. state other than Maryland, or in Washington, D.C., optional analytics and advertising start enabled unless you have saved a rejection. These visits do not show an initial cookie banner. Use Cookie settings at any time to turn either category off or reject both. This default is recorded separately from a choice you make yourself; visiting does not accept purchase terms or subscribe you to messages.

For Maryland, outside the United States, or when the location check is unavailable, optional tracking starts off until you choose to enable it. A default from an earlier U.S. visit does not count as consent for these visits. The location check uses approximate country and state information from our hosting providers. If the webinar host cannot supply it, your browser requests a cookie-free policy from our SootyEdge website on Vercel, without a referrer, account details, or browser identifiers. The policy response contains only the tracking mode and privacy-signal status. We do not ask for precise location or save country or state in your cookie preference.

Global Privacy Control from your browser or its request keeps advertising off. Turning advertising off opts out of advertising sharing by the configured Meta pixel. You can also reject analytics separately. Your enabled choices last up to 180 days on this device. Saved rejections remain off until you change them or clear this site’s stored preferences.

Withdrawing analytics stops optional measurement and removes attributed checkout links. This site clears its known optional measurement identifiers when you withdraw a tracking category. When we receive the updated analytics choice, earlier optional checkout references become ineligible for future registration-to-purchase matching. The necessary privacy-binding cookie may remain to enforce that choice. Verified payment, refund, support, and fulfillment records are handled separately.

Necessary storage remembers privacy choices and supports registration, security, and verified payment confirmation. Optional storage includes a random analytics session, campaign attribution, purchase-event deduplication, Google Analytics identifiers, and Meta advertising identifiers when enabled. Browser settings can also delete cookies; deleting this site’s preference means it will apply the regional defaults again.

Retention and requests

We retain registration and measurement records while needed to operate the event, answer requests, and assess the campaign. Contact support@sootyedge.com to request correction, deletion, or an end to event-related contact. Include your registration reference if available. We do not ask for brokerage credentials, payment-card details, or account balances in this form.

Optional paid offers

The class may present optional SootyEdge software and private instruction. Payment is separate and subject to the offer’s terms. Read SootyEdge’s privacy policy for its software, support, and payment processes.
