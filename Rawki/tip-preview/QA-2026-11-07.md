## Remaining integration boundary

The SootyEdge demo uses synthetic candles and illustrative overlays, not the owners' private indicator code or a live feed. The current page does not mount a genuine-data TradingView iframe. Demo prices and the chart information control disclose this limitation.

TradingView widgets cannot host private Pine scripts or export their market data into this chart. Combining genuine market data and the authentic proprietary suite on one independently rendered chart requires the owners' authorized indicator implementation and a compatible market-data source with suitable display rights. The fixed illustrative overlays must be replaced with calculations appropriate to that data, not simply drawn over live candles.

Production payment, activation and messaging services have not been connected by these changes.
