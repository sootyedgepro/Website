Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Champion Credit LLC, operator of SootyEdge, handles personal information collected through sootyedge.com and related email communications.

01

Information we collect

When you request the free checklist, we collect the first name, email address, primary market selection, consent status, consent time, and available connection information such as an IP address.

For support and activation, we save your name, purchase email, TradingView username, issue details, request reference, and notification status. We also retain checkout campaign tags and an opaque browser-session reference to connect checkout activity with verified sales and refunds when payment integration is configured. Campaign tags and the reference are kept in session storage for the browser tab session. Our hosting provider may also process routine technical information, including request time, device or browser details, referring page, and diagnostic or security logs.

We record anonymous checklist funnel events for page views, form starts, successful submissions, and downloads. These events do not contain names, email addresses, market selections, form values, or advertising cookies.

02

How we use information

  • Deliver the requested checklist and related educational emails.
  • Maintain consent, unsubscribe, and suppression records.
  • Operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website.
  • Respond to support, privacy, and account requests.
  • Meet applicable legal, compliance, and fraud prevention duties.
03

Email choices

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time by using the unsubscribe link included in each message. You may also contact us at support@sootyedge.com. We may retain a limited suppression record so we can honor your choice.

04

Service providers and disclosures

We use service providers that process information for us, including MailerLite for subscriber and email automation services, Airtable for request and sales-event records, Resend for support confirmations and notifications, Stripe for checkout and payments, and Vercel for website hosting and delivery. They may process information only as needed to provide their contracted services and support security and compliance.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, investigate misuse, or complete a business reorganization. We do not sell personal information or share it for behavioral advertising across different contexts.

05

Retention and security

Mailing list information is generally retained while you remain subscribed and afterward only as needed for suppression, security, dispute, and legal records. Operational logs are retained according to our providers' and our operational needs.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including encrypted transmission and restricted access. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

06

Your privacy choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection regarding personal information. You may also withdraw email consent at any time without affecting earlier processing.

Send a request to support@sootyedge.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. You may also have the right to contact your local privacy regulator.

07

International processing

SootyEdge operates from the United States. Our providers may process information in the United States and other countries, which may have different data protection rules than your location.

08

Children

The website and its educational trading materials are not directed to children under 18. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

09

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or practices change. The effective date below identifies the current version. Material changes will be presented through an appropriate website or email notice.

Questions or requests?
